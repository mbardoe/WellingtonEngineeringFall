# Engineering Design Challenge: Zipline Transporter

## Overview
Your mission is to design and build a device that can safely and slowly carry a small figurine (such as a rubber duck) from the top of a zipline (a fishing line) to the bottom. The goal is for your transporter to descend as **slowly** as possible, without stopping or reversing direction.

---

## Constraints
- You may use only materials provided by the teacher.
- Your design must hold a small figurine securely.
- The transporter must move solely due to gravity. No motors or manual pushing.
- The zipline will be made from monofilament fishing line set at a consistent angle.
- The transporter must stay on the line for the entire descent.
- Maximum size: must fit inside a box that is 6" x 6" x 6".

---

## Materials Provided
- Cardboard
- String
- Paper clips
- Tape
- Binder clips
- popsicle sticks

(You may request other simple craft materials as needed.)

---

## Deliverables
1. **Sketches & Notes** in your engineering notebook.
2. **Final Transporter** tested on the class zipline.
3. **Reflection** (1 paragraph): What design decisions helped or hurt your final time? What would you improve with more time?

---

## Competition Day
Each team will get **two test runs** on the class zipline. Your **best time** will be recorded.

---

## Grading Rubric (20 points total)

| Category            | Excellent (5)                             | Good (4)                         | Satisfactory (3)                  | Needs Work (1–2)                     |
|---------------------|--------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Design Thinking** | Innovative design; thoughtful iteration    | Solid design with minor flaws    | Basic functionality achieved      | Minimal planning or ineffective design |
| **Functionality**   | Moves smoothly & slowly; consistent        | Moves with minor issues          | Works but has flaws               | Fails or gets stuck repeatedly        |
| **Notebook Sketches** | Detailed, labeled drawings with notes    | Mostly complete sketches         | Somewhat clear or incomplete      | Few or no meaningful sketches         |
| **Reflection**      | Insightful analysis & thoughtful suggestions | Clear reflection with minor gaps | Some attempt at reflection        | Incomplete or vague response          |

---

## Bonus Challenge
Achieve the **slowest time** of the class without stopping! Winners will be announced and displayed.

---

*Have fun, be creative, and think like an engineer!*
