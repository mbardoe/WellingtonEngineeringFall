# Engineering Careers Presentation (First Draft)

## Overview
Engineering has many branches, each with unique challenges, skills, and contributions to society.  
In this assignment, you will research **one field of engineering** and create the **first draft** of a short presentation about it.  

This is a **draft assignment** — you are not presenting yet. Later in the term, we will return to this project, revise your work, and then you will give your final presentation to the class.

---


## Instructions

1. **Choose a field of engineering** to research. Examples include:
   - Civil Engineering
   - Mechanical Engineering
   - Electrical Engineering
   - Computer Engineering
   - Aerospace Engineering
   - Chemical Engineering
   - Biomedical Engineering
   - Environmental Engineering
   - (Or another field approved by your teacher)

2. **Research your field.** Use at least two reliable sources (websites, articles, videos, or books).  
   Write down the sources you used and include them at the end of your slides.

3. **Create a draft presentation** (PowerPoint, Google Slides, or similar).  
   Aim for **6–8 slides**, including:

   ### Slide 1: Title Slide
   - Your name  
   - Chosen field of engineering  

   ### Slide 2: What Engineers Do
   - Main responsibilities in this field  
   - Key skills or tools they use  

   ### Slide 3: Example Project
   - One project, invention, or product from this field  
   - Why it was important or innovative  
   - Include at least one image  

   ### Slide 4: Education & Training
   - Typical college degrees or certifications  
   - Skills needed for success  

   ### Slide 5: Impact on Society
   - How this field improves people’s lives  
   - Current or future challenges engineers face  

   ### Slide 6: Personal Connection
   - Do you find this field interesting? Why or why not?  
   - Would you consider it as a career?  

   ### Slide 7–8: Sources
   - List your sources (at least two)  
   - Include image credits if needed  

---

## Deliverable
- A **first draft presentation** (6–8 slides) saved as PowerPoint (.pptx) or Google Slides (shared link).  
- Submit your draft by the beginning of the next class.  
- You will **not present yet**. This draft will be improved later in the course before the final presentation.

## Grading Rubric (20 points total)

This draft will be graded mainly on **completion and effort**, not polish.  
Your final presentation later will be graded more strictly for design and delivery.

| Category             | Excellent (5)                               | Good (4)                          | Satisfactory (3)                     | Needs Work (1–2)                   |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **Content**          | Accurate, detailed, and clearly explained    | Mostly accurate with minor gaps   | Some details missing or vague         | Incomplete or inaccurate           |
| **Example Project**  | Well-chosen, explained, and illustrated      | Adequate example with some detail | Example weakly explained or unclear   | No example provided                |
| **Organization**     | Slides follow logical structure with headings | Mostly organized, minor issues   | Weak structure or missing sections    | Disorganized or very incomplete    |
| **Personal Connection** | Thoughtful and reflective                 | Clear but surface-level           | Minimal effort or vague reflection    | Missing or irrelevant              |
| **Sources**          | At least two, properly cited                 | Two sources with minor citation gaps | One source or weak citations        | No sources cited                   |
