# Day 3 Class Notes

## Check-in on Careers Presentations (5 min)

- Quick reminder that these are **drafts**, not polished presentations.  
- Ask students to share one surprising or interesting fact they’ve found so far about their chosen engineering field.

## OnShape Work Session (30 min)

- Continue with the Cake build in OnShape, following the tutorial playlist.  
- Focus on:  
  - Creating sketches and applying constraints  
  - Using the extrude feature  
- Teacher circulates to provide help and troubleshoot.  
- Pause midway for a quick "show and tell" — students share progress on their screen with neighbors.

## Reflection (10 min)

- Students respond to the following questions (on paper or electronically):  
  - *What’s one feature in OnShape that felt powerful or useful today?*  
  - *What’s one thing that was confusing or challenging?*  

- Share out a few responses with the class.
