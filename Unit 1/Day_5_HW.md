
# Engineering Careers Presentation (Draft 2)

## Overview

## Instructions

1. **Review Draft 1**
   - Look back over your original slides (Slides 1–8).  
   - Make small improvements if needed (better wording, clearer images, more detail).  
   - If your perspective on the field has changed since your first draft, update your *Personal Connection* slide.

2. **Add Five New Slides**
   Add the following slides to your presentation:

   ### Slide 9: Tools & Technology
   - Show specific software, machines, or methods engineers in this field rely on.  
   - Include at least one image.

   ### Slide 10: Current Issues or Challenges
   - What problems are engineers in this field currently working to solve?  
   - Example: climate change, cybersecurity, renewable energy, medical device ethics.

   ### Slide 11: Famous Engineer or Breakthrough
   - Highlight one person (past or present) who made an important contribution in this field.  
   - Or describe a major breakthrough invention.

   ### Slide 12: Future Directions
   - What are emerging trends, technologies, or possibilities in this field?  
   - Example: AI, space exploration, renewable energy.

   ### Slide 13: Expanded Sources
   - Add any new sources you used for Draft 2.  
   - Make sure you have **at least three total sources** across both drafts.  
   - Include image credits if needed.
