# Homework: Explore the Drawing Toolbar in OnShape

In our last class, you learned how to create a basic drawing from one of your parts.  
The **Drawing toolbar** has many additional tools that engineers use to make their drawings clearer and more useful.  

For this homework, you will **research one tool** from the Drawing toolbar that we did *not* cover in class, and prepare a short explanation for your classmates.

---

## Instructions

1. **Pick a Tool**  
   - Open the Drawing workspace in OnShape.  
   - Look at the toolbar and choose **one tool** that we did not use in class.  
   - Examples include:  
     - Section View  
     - Broken View  
     - Detail View  
     - Auxiliary View  
     - Hole Callout  
     - Surface Finish  
     - Geometric Tolerances  

2. **Research It**  
   - Try using the tool on one of your parts.  
   - Look for help on OnShape’s support pages, forums, or YouTube.  
   - Figure out what the tool does and why an engineer might need it.  

3. **Prepare a Mini-Presentation (2–3 minutes)**  
   - Show:  
     1. What the tool does  
     2. How to use it in OnShape  
     3. Why it would matter in real engineering work  
   - You may demonstrate directly in OnShape or show screenshots.  

---

## Deliverable
- A **2–3 minute tutorial presentation** in the next class.  
- Be ready to show your classmates how your chosen tool works.  

---

## Challenge (Optional)
- Try more than one tool and compare them.  
- Create a drawing that combines at least two advanced tools (for example: a Section View with a Hole Callout).
