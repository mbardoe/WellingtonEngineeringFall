# OnShape Homework: Design a Dice

For this assignment, you will design a standard **dice (singular of dice is "die")** in OnShape.  
This will give you practice with **sketches, extrusions, text, and fillets**.

---

## Requirements

1. **Base Shape**
   - Create a cube that is **5 cm × 5 cm × 5 cm**.

2. **Numbers**
   - You may represent the numbers in **one of three ways**:
     - Numerals (1, 2, 3, …)  *Easy*
     - Number words (“one,” “two,” “three,” …)  *Medium*
     - Pips (the standard dots used on dice)  *Hard*

3. **Layout Rule**
   - Opposite sides must add up to **7**:
     - 1 is opposite 6  
     - 2 is opposite 5  
     - 3 is opposite 4  

4. **Fillets**
   - Add rounded edges to your cube using the **Fillet tool**.  
   - Your dice should look like a real game die with smooth corners.

---

## Tips

- Use **Sketch → Text** to add numerals or words.  
- Use **Sketch → Circle** for pips.  
- Use **Extrude → Remove** to engrave, or **Extrude → Add** to emboss.  
- Remember: your cube must stay 5 cm after fillets (don’t scale it down accidentally).  
- Check that all faces are labeled correctly before finishing.

---

## Deliverable

- Your finished dice model saved as an OnShape **Document**.  
- **Name your document**: `Lastname_Dice`
