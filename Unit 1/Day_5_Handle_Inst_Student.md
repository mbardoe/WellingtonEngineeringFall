# OnShape Project: Modeling the Scissors Handle

In this part of the project, you will design the **handle** of the scissors.  
This will give you more practice with **sketching, extrude, fillet, and sweep**.

---

## Step 1: Start a New Sketch
1. Create a new **sketch** on the **Top plane**.  
2. Use the **origin** as your starting reference point.  
3. Sketch the **general outline of the handle** using circles and arcs.  
   - One large circle for the finger hole.  
   - One smaller circle for the thumb hole.  
   - Connect them with arcs to form the overall handle shape.  

*Reference image for handle sketch:*  
![Handle Sketch Reference](img/handle.png.png)

---

## Step 2: Dimension the Sketch
1. Dimension the **diameter** of each circle (finger hole should be larger than the thumb hole).  
2. Add distances between holes and arcs to fully define the shape.  
3. Make sure your handle shape matches the proportions of the reference image.  

---

## Step 3: Extrude the Handle
1. Finish the sketch.  
2. Use **Extrude** to create the handle thickness (same thickness as the blade: **1.4 mm**).  
3. Rename the part **Handle**.  

---

## Step 4: Add Fillets
1. Use the **Fillet tool** on the **outer edges** of the handle.  
2. This will smooth out the sharp edges and make it feel more realistic.  

---

## Step 5: Hollow Out the Finger Holes
1. Select the **inner circles** from your original sketch.  
2. Use **Extrude → Remove** to cut out the finger and thumb holes.  
3. Check from different views to make sure the holes cut all the way through.

---


## Deliverable
- A completed **scissors handle model** with holes and filleted edges.  
- Save your OnShape document as:  
  - `Lastname_ScissorsHandle`
