# Homework Assignment: Create a Hinge in OnShape

## Objective
Your task is to design and model a **hinge** in OnShape. This assignment builds on the skills you used for the scissors project, including sketching, extruding, creating holes, and assembling moving parts.

---

## Requirements

1. **Hinge Plates**
   - Create two rectangular plates (about 40 mm × 20 mm × 3 mm each).
   - Add a pivot cylinder on one side of each plate so that they interlock when assembled. The hing should be able to 
close without overlapping.
   - Each cylinder should have a **hole through the center** for a pin.

2. **Pin**
   - Create a pin (or rod) that fits through the holes of both hinge plates.
   - The pin should allow the plates to rotate around it.

3. **Assembly**
   - Import the two plates and the pin into an OnShape Assembly.
   - Use **Revolute Mates** so that the plates rotate around the pin.
   - Make sure the plates can open and close like a real hinge.

4. **Appearance**
   - Apply appearances (e.g., metal color for the pin, different colors for the plates).

---

## Deliverables

- **Part Studio** with both plates and the pin.
- **Assembly** showing the hinge working.
- **Animation (Optional Bonus):** Create a short animation showing the hinge opening and closing.

---

## Tips

- Make sure dimensions are reasonable (the pin should actually fit through the holes!).  
- If you finish early, experiment with adding fillets, chamfers, or rounded edges for realism.
- If you need support you can watch the video at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cs7QjwOaowE
