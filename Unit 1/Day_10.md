# Day 10 Project: Reverse Engineering & Redesigning a Catapult

In this project, you will use **calipers** to measure a real catapult built by previous students, create a **CAD model** of it in OnShape, and then design your own improved version.

---

## Step 1: Measure the Catapult
1. Get a set of **digital calipers** and one of the class catapults.  
2. Carefully measure each part:  
   - Length, width, and thickness of beams  
   - Positions of holes and pivots  
   - Diameter of rods or pins  
   - Dimensions of the throwing arm and base  
3. Record all measurements in a table (paper or spreadsheet).  
   - Keep measurements consistent (millimeters).

## Step 2: Create a CAD Model (Reverse Engineering)
1. In OnShape, create a **new document** called `Lastname_Catapult_Reverse`.  
2. Model the catapult parts using your measurements.  
   - Base pieces  
   - Uprights  
   - Throwing arm  
   - Pins or rods  
3. Assemble the parts in the **Assembly tab** using mates so it matches the real catapult.  
4. Check proportions and motion against the physical model.  

---

## Step 3: Redesign Your Catapult
1. Create a **new Part Studio** for your custom design.  
2. Start with the same basic structure as the reverse-engineered version.  
3. Modify at least **two features** to make it your own:  
   - Change the size or proportions  
   - Add reinforcements or decorative elements  
   - Alter the throwing arm length or pivot placement  
   - Improve stability of the base  
4. Assemble your redesigned catapult.  
5. Test the motion in OnShape.  

---

## Step 4: Reflection
Answer the following questions (short written response):  
- What design choices did the original catapult make?  
- What changes did you make in your redesign, and why?  
- If you were to build your design in real life, what challenges might you face?  

---

## Deliverables
- **Reverse-engineered CAD model** of the original catapult (`Lastname_Catapult_Reverse`)  
- **Redesigned catapult model** (`Lastname_Catapult_Custom`)  
- **Reflection write-up** (1–2 paragraphs)
