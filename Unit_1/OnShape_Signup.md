# How to Sign Up for OnShape as a Student

OnShape is a professional Computer-Aided Design (CAD) program that runs in your web browser.  
Follow these steps to set up your free **Student/Education account**.

---

## Step 1: Go to OnShape Education
- Open a web browser and go to: [https://www.onshape.com/en/education](https://www.onshape.com/en/education)  
- Click **"Get Started"** under **Students & Educators**.

---

## Step 2: Create an Account
- You will be asked to **sign up with an email address**.  
- **Important:** Use your **school email address** if you have one.  
- Fill out the form with your:
  - First and last name  
  - Email address  
  - Country  
  - School name (enter your school’s full name)  
  - Graduation year  

---

## Step 3: Verify Your Email
- OnShape will send you an email.  
- Open it and click the verification link to activate your account.  
- If you don’t see it, check your spam/junk folder.

---

## Step 4: Log In
- Go to [https://cad.onshape.com](https://cad.onshape.com).  
- Enter your email and password to log in.  
- The first time you log in, select **Education Plan** when asked about your subscription type.

---

## Step 5: Confirm Education Access
- OnShape may ask you to confirm you are a student:  
  - Sometimes they request a school email or other proof.  
  - If so, follow the instructions provided.  

---

## Step 6: Explore
- Once you are logged in, you’ll see your **OnShape Dashboard**.  
- Click **Create → Document** to start your first project.  
- You’re ready to begin designing!

---

## Troubleshooting
- If you can’t verify your email or have issues signing up, try:  
  - Using a different browser (Chrome works best).  
  - Making sure you typed your email correctly.  
  - Contacting OnShape support through their [Help page](https://www.onshape.com/en/contact).  
- If you’re stuck, let your teacher know and we’ll solve it together.
