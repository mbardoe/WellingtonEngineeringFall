# Day 4 Class Notes

## Warm-Up (5 min)

- Quick recap of OnShape tools used so far (extrude, fillet, etc.).  
- Ask: *What’s one OnShape feature you’ve found most useful so far?*  
- Preview today’s focus: **Chamfer, Text, and Revolve tools**.

---

## Cake Project – Adding Detail (55 min)

### Part 1: Text Tool (15 min)
- Demonstrate how to use the **Text tool** in a sketch.  
- Show how to **extrude remove** to engrave text into a surface.  
- Students practice by removing letters from the side of one cake layer.

### Part 2: Chamfer Tool (15 min)
- Demonstrate how to apply **Chamfers** to edges of parts.  
- Students create a new set of decorative columns and add chamfers to their edges for a polished look.  
- Emphasize the difference between chamfers and fillets.

### Part 3: Revolve Tool (20 min)
- Introduce the **Revolve** feature.  
- Demonstrate creating a dome by sketching a half-profile and revolving it around an axis.  
- Students add a dome to the top of their cake.

### Ongoing Teacher Role
- Circulate and provide troubleshooting.  
- Encourage students to pause and show progress at each stage.

---

## Reflection (10 min)

- Students write short responses to:  
  - *Which of today’s tools felt easiest to use?*  
  - *Which tool felt most powerful or flexible?*  
  - *Where might you see these tools applied in real engineering design?*  

- Share out a few examples as a class.

---

## Closing (5 min)

- Highlight how today’s tools (chamfer, text, revolve) add detail and realism to CAD models.  
- Remind students that learning CAD is about building up a toolbox of techniques to solve different design problems.
