# Engineering Notebook Prompts

* What problem are you trying to solve, and why is it important?

* List at least three design ideas you considered. What are the pros and cons of each?

* Sketch your current design and label the major parts.

* What constraints (materials, time, tools, etc.) are influencing your decisions today?

* Describe a design change you made today and explain your reasoning.

* What tool or software did you use today, and what did you learn about it?

* What was one challenge you encountered today, and how did you try to solve it?

* Did anything not go as planned? What might you do differently next time?

* How do you know your design is working (or not working)? What are you using to test it?

* Explain how your team is working together. What’s working well? What needs to improve?

* What feedback did you receive, and how did it influence your next steps?

* Take a photo or screenshot of your design. What do you notice when you look at it now?

* Describe one way your design could be improved with a different material or method.

* How are you keeping track of your time and tasks during this project?

* What new skill did you practice or learn today?

* Explain how your design uses at least one mechanical element (hinge, slider, gear, etc.)

* What are you most proud of in your design so far?

* What is still unfinished, unclear, or in progress? What’s your plan to keep moving forward?

* How does your design reflect the needs of the user or audience?

* Now that you’re nearly done, what would you change if you started this project over?
