# Course Goals: Introduction to Engineering

## Exploration Goals
1. **Students will explore a range of engineering fields and career paths**, learning about the skills, education, and real-world applications of each.
2. **Students will reflect on their own interests and strengths** in relation to possible engineering pathways, through discussions, reflections, or brief research assignments.

## Design Process & Journaling Goals
3. **Students will apply the engineering design process**—define, ideate, prototype, test, iterate, communicate—to create a physical object that solves a meaningful problem.
4. **Students will maintain an engineering journal** to document their design thinking, sketches, CAD work, fabrication notes, test results, and reflections.

## Design & Fabrication Goals
5. **Students will develop hands-on skills in design and fabrication**, including the safe and effective use of 3D printers, laser cutter, and vinyl cutter.
6. **Students will complete at least one full-cycle design project**, starting from a problem or need and culminating in a functional prototype or product.

## Technical Skills Goals
7. **Students will develop foundational proficiency in Autodesk Fusion**, including creating parametric sketches, 3D parts, assemblies, and preparing files for fabrication.
8. **Students will use fabrication software** (e.g., PrusaSlicer, Lightburn, Vinyl Express LXI) to process and produce their designs using available tools.
