
# High School Engineering Course: Unit Breakdown

This course meets 31 times for approximately 1 hour per session. Students explore engineering careers, learn CAD using Autodesk Fusion, and complete fabrication projects using tools in the school's Maker Space.

---

## Unit 1: Introduction to Engineering + Career Exploration (Days 1–4)

**Goals:**
- Understand what engineering is
- Learn about major disciplines and job opportunities
- Intro to problem-solving process

**Key Activities:**
- Slides/notes/discussions on engineering fields
- Personality & interest inventory → “Which type of engineer are you?”
- Group challenge: “Paper Tower Design Challenge”
- Begin engineering notebooks or digital journals

**Deliverable:**  
Short reflection on careers + sketch of an engineering-related project idea

---

## Unit 2: CAD & Digital Design Foundations (Days 5–10)

**Goals:**
- Learn OnShape basics: sketches, constraints, extrude, revolve, fillets
- Understand tolerances and parametric design
- Apply CAD to a simple physical product

**Key Activities:**
- Fusion 360 tutorials and teacher-led demos
- Create a personalized keychain, custom coin, or token

**Deliverable:**  
Export STL, slice with PrusaSlicer, and 3D print the design

---

## ✂️ Unit 3: Laser & Vinyl Cutting Projects (Days 11–16)

**Goals:**
- Learn vector design basics
- Convert designs to cut paths using LightBurn and LXI
- Understand fabrication limits and materials

**Key Activities:**
- Design and cut a custom sign or sticker (vinyl)
- Create a layered nameplate or puzzle with laser cutter
- Learn safety and material setup

**Deliverable:**  
Physical product + short write-up or explanation

---

## ⚙️ Unit 4: Mechanisms & Prototyping (Days 17–24)

**Goals:**
- Learn about gears, cams, levers, and motion
- Combine CAD and fabrication into a working mechanism
- Understand tolerance and fit

**Key Activities:**
- Mini-lessons on mechanisms
- Design and build a crank slider, cam toy, or gearbox
- Optional: Vinyl decoration

**Deliverable:**  
Interactive model + engineering notebook entry

---

## 🚀 Unit 5: Capstone Design Challenge (Days 25–31)

**Goals:**
- Work in teams using the full engineering design process
- Experience the challenges of teamwork, project management, and iteration
- Solve a real problem using CAD and maker tools
- Reflect on learning

**Project Ideas:**
- Desk organizer
- Adaptive tool
- Puzzle box
- Reusable straw case
- “Engineer for Good” challenge

**Deliverable:**  
Prototype + Presentation:
- CAD screenshots
- Photos of fabrication
- Reflection
