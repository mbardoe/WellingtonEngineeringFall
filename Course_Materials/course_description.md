# Engineering: The Design Process, CAD, and 3D printing

Students will explore engineering career opportunities. They will learn and use professional computer aided design and 
drafting software. They will explore the engineering design process through design and fabrication projects.

# Engineering: Electronics and Computer Programming

Students will explore the function and interaction of common electronic devices. They will learn Python programming and 
use it to explore how computers can used to model engineering problems.

# Engineering: AI, Control Systems, and Human-Machine Interfaces

Students will explore how artificial intelligence (AI) and smart control systems allow machines to interact with humans. 
Through hands-on projects, they will design devices that utilize AI-driven object recognition systems and experiment 
with gesture, voice, and vision-based machine interactions.
